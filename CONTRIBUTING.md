# Contributing

This is a community commons. Contributing to it should not require being a
programmer, and there is nothing here you can break that cannot be undone.

There are **three ways in**, in increasing order of technical bar. Pick the lowest
one that fits — nobody is expected to climb.

## 1. An idea, a question, an objection → Discussions

Type it into a box. That is the whole procedure. No review step, no formatting
rules, no need to know where it belongs. If it turns into work, someone will turn
it into an Issue; you do not have to.

Once you have joined a discussion you can **keep participating from your email
inbox** — GitHub will send you replies and anything you write back gets posted.
Many members will never need to visit this site again after their first discussion.

## 2. A page, a guide, a correction → edit the document

Every page has a **pencil icon** at its top right. Click it, make your change, and
GitHub will offer to "propose changes." That opens a suggestion for others to look
at — it does not go live immediately, so there is no way to break the site.

This is how the guides, the charter, and the project descriptions get better. If you
spot something wrong, out of date, or badly explained, fixing it is genuinely
welcome and costs you about a minute.

**Writing conventions,** for anyone who wants them: plain markdown; link between
pages with relative links like `[Projects](projects.md)`; keep one idea per
paragraph and prefer plain words. `[[Wikilinks]]` do not work here.

## 3. Code, feeds, plumbing → pull requests

Standard fork-and-PR. See the [WordPress spec](docs/wordpress-spec.md) for what is
being built and why, and the open Issues for what needs doing. The charter's
principles are binding constraints on implementation, not aspirations — a change
that stores canonical member content here, or that makes a public view by filtering
a private one, will be declined however well it works.

## What we especially need

- **Portuguese translation.** This repository is entirely in English in a
  Portuguese-speaking community. That is the most important accessibility gap here.
- **Plain-language guides** — how to claim a domain, how to publish a profile.
- **Accessibility** review of anything we build.
- **Someone to disagree with us in public.** The fork guarantee is only real if
  people feel free to use it.

## The one rule

Nothing is published about a member that the member did not publish themselves.
If you are adding someone else's project, land, or details, stop — ask them to
publish it at their own address, and we will point at it.
