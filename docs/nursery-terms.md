# The Nursery

> **Draft — not yet in force.** This is the working text of the nursery terms,
> published here so it can be read, argued with and improved in the open before
> anyone is asked to agree to it. **No member has joined the nursery under these
> terms, and nobody should be invited until the four open items below are closed.**
> Tracked in [issue #7](https://github.com/barlavento-eco/barlavento-eco.github.io/issues/7).
>
> Still open: **(1)** how long the exit redirect lasts — a community decision;
> **(2)** competent legal review of the copyright licence (section 3) and the data
> commitments (section 7), since MVF is a Portuguese nonprofit holding EU
> residents' personal data; **(3)** a Portuguese translation, and a decision on
> which language governs; **(4)** MVF's agreement to the closure notice in
> section 8. Unresolved points are marked **[Open]** in the text rather than
> hidden.

If you don't have a website yet, the nursery is where you can start publishing
straight away — today, with no site of your own, no cost, and no technical setup.

It is called a nursery on purpose. It is where a seedling grows until it is ready
to be planted in its own ground. It is a good place to start. It is not meant to
be where you stay.

This page tells you exactly what you get, what you keep, and how you leave. Please
read it before you join — particularly the part about leaving, because that is the
part that makes the rest trustworthy.

## 1. What the nursery is

A space on the barlavento.eco website where you can publish posts under your own
name. Your posts appear at `barlavento.eco/author/your-name/` and flow into the
[River](river.md) alongside everything published by members who have their own
websites.

**The ground is ours; what grows on it is yours.** We host the page. We do not own
your words, and we cannot stop you taking them with you. See section 3.

We chose a path (`barlavento.eco/author/your-name/`) rather than a web address that
looks like your own (`your-name.barlavento.eco`) deliberately. The second looks like
land you own, and it isn't — it is ours, and if you left, it would either stop
working or we would have to keep it alive forever. We would rather be honest about
whose ground you are standing on, so that the day you move onto your own is a real
step and not a technicality.

**Want something that is genuinely yours from day one?** Buy a domain name — usually
around €10–15 a year — and we will help you point it at your nursery page for free.
Then `your-farm.pt` is the address people learn, you own it outright, and the day
you move to your own website nothing breaks and nobody has to update a link. You do
not need to do this to join. It is just the best €12 you can spend on this.

## 2. What you get

- An account you log into, to write and publish posts whenever you want
- Your own page listing everything you have written
- Your own feed, so people can subscribe to you specifically
- Your posts carried in the River, and your name in the community Directory
- Help getting started — a person, not a manual

**You publish. Nobody approves your posts first.** What you write goes live when you
press the button. We are not an editor and we do not want to be one. The community's
voice is not something the website's caretakers curate.

## 3. What you keep — your words are yours

**You keep the copyright in everything you write and every photograph you upload.**
Joining the nursery does not transfer ownership of anything to us.

By publishing here you give us permission to display your posts on barlavento.eco,
include them in the River, and share them in the community's feeds and newsletters.
That permission is limited to that purpose, and it ends when you leave — except for
material already sent out (an email newsletter already delivered cannot be recalled,
and archives of past River issues stay as they were).

We will not sell your work, license it to anyone else, put advertising on it, or use
it to train anything.

**You can take everything with you at any time, without asking and without giving a
reason.** See section 4.

## 4. Leaving

You can leave whenever you like. Here is exactly what happens.

**Your content.** We give you a complete export file of everything you have written,
including your photographs, in a standard format that imports directly into any
WordPress site — and can be converted for other systems. You can ask for this export
at any time, including when you have no intention of leaving. It is yours on request.

**Your readers.** For a fixed period after you move, your nursery feed automatically
forwards to your new one, so the people subscribed to you carry over without doing
anything. After that period the forwarding stops.

> **[Open]** How long that period runs is not yet decided. Twelve months has been
> proposed. This belongs to the community meeting, not to whoever writes this page,
> and the agreed figure will be stated here before anyone joins.

We put a limit on it deliberately, and it is worth explaining why. A promise to
maintain something forever, for people who have left, is a promise that quietly
turns this project into a landlord to its own former members. We would rather make
a smaller promise and keep it. **This is also why we recommend buying a domain in
section 1 — if the address is yours, the forwarding question never arises at all.**

**The order matters.** Export your content *before* we close your account, because
the export needs your photographs to still be online to collect them. We will always
do it in that order, and we will not close an account without telling you first.

**Your old page.** Once you have moved, `barlavento.eco/author/your-name/` stops
being your home. Tell us where you want it to point and we will point it there for
the same period as the feed.

## 5. What we ask of you

Only what you would expect:

- **Publish your own work.** Your words, your photos, or material you have the right
  to use. If you quote someone, say who.
- **Keep it connected to why we are here** — ecosystem regeneration in the Barlavento,
  the work you are doing, what you are learning, what you need. This is not a rule
  about tone or topic narrowness; write about your soil, your failures, your neighbours,
  your hopes. It is a rule against using the nursery as free hosting for something
  unrelated.
- **Don't post anything about another person that they haven't agreed to** — this
  community's whole approach to data is that nothing appears about anyone unless they
  put it there themselves. That applies to you as much as to us.

## 6. What we can do, and what we won't

**We will not edit your posts.** If we think something needs changing, we will ask you.

We can unpublish content in three situations only: it is unlawful; it is not yours to
publish; or it breaks section 5. If that happens we will tell you which one and why,
in writing, before or immediately after — and you keep your export either way. If you
think we got it wrong, the community's meeting decides, not the person who pressed the
button.

We cannot see your drafts before you publish them, and we will not go looking.

## 7. Your information

To give you an account we hold your name, your email address, and your login details.
That is all. We do not track your reading, we do not run advertising, and we do not
pass anything to anyone else.

- **To see what we hold about you, or to correct it:** ask, and we will show you.
- **To have it deleted:** ask, and we will delete your account and your personal
  details.
- **One thing to understand about deletion:** posts you have already published are
  public statements to a community, and they stay published unless you also ask us to
  remove them — which you can. Deleting your account and unpublishing your writing are
  two separate requests, and you can make either or both.

> **[Open]** MVF is the data controller here and this section carries obligations
> under EU data-protection law. The commitments above are the ones we intend to make;
> someone competent should confirm the wording discharges them before these terms
> take effect.

## 8. If the nursery closes

We are volunteers. This project could end, or change shape, or run out of people.
It would be strange to promise you a clean exit and say nothing about ours.

If the nursery is going to close, we will tell every member at least **three months**
in advance, in writing, and we will send everyone their export file at the same time
rather than waiting to be asked. Feed forwarding for anyone who has already moved
continues to the end of its term or the end of the site, whichever comes first.

Everything this project builds — the site, the code, the templates — is public and
openly licensed, so if we stop and you want to carry it on, you can. That is not a
consolation prize; it is the point.

> **[Open]** This commits the project to a notice period somebody has to be around to
> honour. It needs MVF's agreement, not just the agreement of whoever drafted it.

## 9. Changes to these terms

If we change anything here, we will tell members before it takes effect and say what
changed and why. Changes affecting what you keep or how you leave (sections 3, 4 and
8) go to the community meeting first. We are not going to quietly weaken the exit
guarantee — it is the part that makes everything else worth trusting.

This page lives in a public repository, so every change to it is on the record with a
date and an author. You do not have to take our word for it: you can read the history.

## 10. Questions

Ask in the [Discussions](https://github.com/barlavento-eco/barlavento-eco.github.io/discussions).

> **[Open]** A way to ask that does not require a GitHub account. The people most
> likely to need the nursery are the least likely to open a GitHub thread to ask
> about it, so this probably needs to be an email address or a named person.
