# Ecosystem Regeneration Projects

> **Ported 2026-07-22** from [mudvalley.org](https://mudvalley.org/get-involved/ecosystem-regeneration-community-of-the-barlavento/ecosystem-regeneration-projects/), verbatim, as the starting content for the commons. **This is a transitional copy.** Under the [charter](../README.md), barlavento.eco does not hold the canonical description of any member project — each steward's own site does. The intended end state is that every entry below shrinks to a pointer at a profile the project publishes itself. See [Directory](#from-this-page-to-the-directory) at the foot of this page.

A dynamic collection of ecosystem regeneration projects in the western Algarve is joining together to scale our impact.

## Meet the changemakers!

Each participating ecosystem regeneration project in the Ecosystem Regeneration Community of the Barlavento is the confluence of a location, people, and actions working to assure improved soil quality, water availability, biodiversity, local livelihoods, and inspiration in the Barlavento region of Portugal.

### Quinta Vale da Lama

**Location:** Odiáxere

A 43-hectare farm applying regenerative agriculture and ecosystem restoration methodologies in a collaborative setting to improve soil quality, water availability, biodiversity, and local livelihoods. Key landscape areas include an organic market garden, dry fruit orchard, syntropic food forest, composting operation, and a variety of semi-arid zones undergoing restoration. Two nature- and regeneration-focused nonprofits are located here, as is the Casa Vale da Lama eco-resort.

[Learn more](https://www.valedalama.net/) about Quinta Vale da Lama

### Vinha Velha

**Location:** Barão de São João

A diverse, 140 hectare, 2nd-generation organic farm dedicated to sustainable and regenerative living and working practices. The farming operation cultivates various types of vegetables throughout the year using the closed-farm circle concept, and animals on the farm are raised following biodynamic principles. A number of reforestation and landscape resiliency efforts are being undertaken around the property. The project also features holiday accommodations as well as educational programs.

[Learn more](https://www.vinhavelha.com/) about Vinha Velha

### Quinta do Jasmim

**Location:** Bensafrim

An educative and productive project aiming to infuse an interest to health resiliency, offering homegrown herbal medicines to the local community as well as a program of herbal workshops for children and adults. The Mediterranean medicine garden is a conscious impulse to Biodiversity in an arid bushland area.

[Learn more](https://www.mamaseednaturals.com/) about Quinta do Jasmim

### Restore Nature Foundation

**Location:** Barão de São João

Working to regenerate native ecosystems in southern Portugal to make landscapes resilient, productive, and vibrant by increasing biodiversity, improving soil conditions, and restoring the water cycle. The Foundation works on holistic landscape regeneration and offers education and services that derive from its members experiences with regenerative frameworks and techniques to bring life back to degrading ecosystems.

[Learn more](https://restorenaturecollective.com/) about Restore Nature Foundation

### Santuário da Machada

**Location:** Bensafrim

Focuses on the ecological and hydrological regeneration of a 13-hectare wild cork oak and medronho forest located on the upper slopes of the Ribeira de Bensafrim catchment. Surrounded by different examples of extractive land use and for decades damaged by cork oak machinery, the site is now part of a growing collective effort to restore the water cycle and envision a new and broader perspective on land use.

### Quinta dos Três Figos

**Location:** Marmelete

An organic farm project featuring a 4-hectare diversified fruit orchard and an additional 16-hectares of ecosystem regeneration works, with plans to produce vegetables and extensive goat breeding for fire prevention.

### African Peace Village

**Location:** Burgau

Creates sustainable business plans as opportunities for potential impact investments, addresses social issues at scale, and enhances biotic relationships between Africa and the World devoid of supremacy. Seeks novel metrics for social and ecological impact, and offers practical hands-on learning and field trips to other local regenerative projects in Portugal.

[Learn more](https://africanway.world/) about African Peace Village

### Zambujeira de Fora

**Location:** Barão de São João

A 1.2-hectare family project located in the flat lands of Barão de São João, focusing on quality of life and ecosystem restoration. The land has a few different ecosystems, including an esteval on the higher ground, a sobreiral on the lower part, and bushy areas scattered around open spaces. The project engages in experimental successional agroforestry, with a focus on increasing plant diversity and density.

The project takes its name from the zambujeira, the wild olive tree found throughout the Algarve and Portugal.

### Quinta do Horizonte

**Location:** Pedralva

An 8.5-hectare project centered around the holy trinity of water, soil, and plants. It is a place for connection and regeneration with nature, where we honor the life processes that are the foundation of our world. It brings life back to degrading landscapes through an approach that is embedded in science and decades of practical experience by hundreds of visionaries from all over the world.

### Corte Medronheira

**Location:** Bensafrim

A 36 hectare land mainly covered with cork oaks, strawberry trees and pine trees. An extended family of kindred spirits, adults and children, willing to experience, listen, learn and embody regenerative experiences in an immersive and authentic way. This place is a temple, a sanctuary, a place of retreat and rest for those who arrive exhausted by modernity, a place to (re)learn what it means to belong and exist as part of a living and complex ecosystem.

[Learn more](https://www.instagram.com/medronheira_em_rede/) about Corte Medronheira

### Mata Viva

**Location:** Barão de São João

A volunteer movement for participative post-fire intervention in the public area of the Barão de São João National Forest. The movement was formed after the destruction of the Fall 2025 wildfires in the area. It works with other local ecological regeneration entities and local and national government authorities to support forest restoration as well as the design and implementation of ecological, educational, social, and artistic actions for the local community around Barão de São João.

[Contact](mailto:mataviva2025@gmail.com) Mata Viva to learn more about them

### Lagos Mais Verde

**Location:** Lagos

A citizens' group promoting the creation and preservation of green spaces in Lagos in order to foster community well-being, environmental education, and urban regeneration. This movement was born out of a common desire to make Lagos a greener, healthier and more inclusive city – with natural spaces accessible to all ages.

[Learn more](https://www.lagosmaisverde.org/english) about Lagos Mais Verde

## From this page to the Directory

This page is a **snapshot in the old shape** — one organisation describing twelve others on a page it controls. The charter's second principle says content lives at addresses members control, and its third says a profile *is* consent. So this page is the starting point, not the destination.

Read against the [growth ladder](../README.md#the-growth-path-seedling--sapling--established), the twelve projects already sort themselves:

| Stage | Projects | What the commons owes them |
|---|---|---|
| 🌳 **Established** — own domain, own site | Quinta Vale da Lama, Vinha Velha, Quinta do Jasmim, Restore Nature Foundation, African Peace Village, Lagos Mais Verde | Nothing but a pointer and a feed read. Ask them to publish a profile; index it. |
| 🌿 **Sapling** — a presence, but on rented ground | Corte Medronheira (Instagram) | Offer the transplant: a domain of their own, content carried over. |
| 🌱 **Seedling** — no independent web presence found | Santuário da Machada, Quinta dos Três Figos, Zambujeira de Fora, Quinta do Horizonte, Mata Viva (email only) | A nursery page here, with export guaranteed — and an invitation to claim a domain. |

**Six of twelve are already sovereign.** That is a much stronger starting position than "we need to build everyone a website," and it sets the first real task: not building pages, but asking six stewards to publish a small profile at an address they already own.

**Nothing above is a commitment on anyone's behalf.** No project is listed in the Directory, and no description is treated as canonical, until that project self-publishes. Until then this page stays exactly what it is: a courtesy copy of what mudvalley.org already says in public.
