# Roadmap

How barlavento.eco is being built, what is coming, and what is still an open
question. This page lives in the same public repository as everything else here —
if it is out of date, that is a bug, and you can say so.

## Start here: tell us you're around

The most useful thing anyone can give this project right now is **an opinion**.
Not code, not a website, not technical skill — a sense of what this should be
for, and what would make it useful to you.

There is a thread for exactly that, pinned at the top of our
[**Discussions**](https://github.com/barlavento-eco/barlavento-eco.github.io/discussions).
Say who you are, what brought you here, and what you'd like this site to do that
it doesn't do yet.

**Escreva em português se preferir.** Both languages are welcome, everywhere.
Nobody should have to discuss their own community in a second language.

You'll need a free GitHub account and nothing else. Once you've joined a thread
you can keep replying **from your email inbox** — many people never visit the
site again after their first post.

## Where we are

The first version of barlavento.eco is deliberately small: a few pages, a map of
who works with whom, and a map of where the projects are.

The architecture it is meant to grow into is described in [the charter](../).
Most of it is not built yet, and this page is an honest account of that rather
than a prospectus.

## The three layers

The charter describes this site as a **confluence** rather than a platform —
three layers that together make a community visible without any one of them
owning it.

| Layer | What it is | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|
| 🗺️ **Directory** | Each member publishes a profile at *their own* web address; this site indexes only what members self-publish | **Partly built.** The Social Map is live. Most entries are still recorded *about* members rather than *by* them — see the open questions |
| 🌊 **River** | Members' own posts flow here by RSS; readers see the whole community, every piece links home to its author | **Not started** |
| 🌰 **Commons** | The public repository: the code, the guides, and the record of every decision | **Built — and this page is it becoming visible from the site for the first time** |

## Next

- **Portuguese.** The community is Portuguese-speaking and this site is in
  English. The project's own spec calls this *"a real accessibility failure, not
  a nice-to-have."* Being worked on now.
- **A privacy policy** that says plainly what this site does and does not
  collect.
- **A community workgroup** — drawn from across the community, not only from the
  volunteers currently doing the work — to decide what a site *of, by and for the
  community* actually means here. **This is the decision that governs everything
  below it, and it has not been made yet.**

## Later

- **The River.** Feed plumbing, so members' own writing appears here
  automatically without anyone copying it.
- **An open API.** Everything this site knows, available to anyone who wants to
  build on it — other bioregions included. The data is already machine-readable;
  an API makes it usable without scraping.
- **The Seedling nursery.** A home for members who don't yet have a site of their
  own, with exit rights guaranteed from the start.
  [Discussion #5](https://github.com/barlavento-eco/barlavento-eco.github.io/discussions/5)
  is open on what form it should take.
- **A shared events calendar** — or a link to one. Neighbouring projects such as
  [baldio.eu](https://baldio.eu) show what a shared agenda does for a community.
  Whether that belongs here or is better linked than rebuilt is genuinely open.

## Open questions

These are undecided, and opinions are wanted. Each is a live discussion.

- **Is barlavento.eco WordPress, a static site, or both?** Not decided. The spec
  warns against letting prototype momentum decide it by default.
- **Who hosts and who administers it?** A dependency on one person contradicts
  *of / for / by*, and needs an answer before launch rather than after.
- **How do members get onto the map?** Today the site waits for members to
  publish their own profiles — and so far, very few have. Other directories let
  anyone add an entry and let the subject claim it afterwards. Which of those
  this community wants is a real choice with real trade-offs.
- **What licence?** Proposed: MIT for the code, CC BY-SA 4.0 for text and
  templates. **Not yet ratified** — and until it is, the promise that you can
  fork this project is not one we can actually keep.

## How to change any of this

[Open a discussion](https://github.com/barlavento-eco/barlavento-eco.github.io/discussions).
No account with us, nothing to install, no technical skill — a GitHub account and
a box to type in. [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) describes the three ways in,
in increasing order of technical bar. Pick the lowest one that fits.
